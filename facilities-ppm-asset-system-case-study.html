Anonymised project example

Facilities PPM and asset management system

An anonymised project model for facilities teams that need maintenance history, assets, PPM and compliance evidence to behave like one system.

The challenge

Facilities information often grows organically: an asset spreadsheet, a PPM sheet, contractor PDFs in email and defects in somebody's notebook. Each part works until somebody needs the whole picture.

The Forge approach

  • Create a site and location hierarchy
  • Build asset records with service history
  • Link PPM tasks to equipment and locations
  • Track reactive defects and repair history
  • Connect contractor documents and compliance evidence

The operational benefit

A single asset can show what it is, where it is, what maintenance is due, what has failed before and where its supporting documentation sits.

Privacy note: This example describes the structure and workflow used in this type of project. Client-identifying information is deliberately excluded.
