What we offer

Maintenance support without the usual drama

Services are tailored to the site and agreed scope. Specialist or regulated work is referred to appropriately qualified contractors where required, because confidence is useful but pretending is expensive.

Reactive maintenance

📅

Planned maintenance

Routine checks, seasonal tasks, maintenance schedules, inspection rounds and completion records.

Hospitality support

Managed property support

Small works

Asset registers, PPM planners, SOPs, job records, inspection sheets and site-specific compliance documents.
