PPM checklist for commercial buildings

A practical checklist for building a planned preventive maintenance system that shows what needs doing, when, by whom and what evidence proves it happened.

  • Create a current asset and system register
  • Assign maintenance and inspection frequencies
  • Identify statutory or competent-person tasks
  • Record responsible person or contractor
  • Track due dates and overdue actions
  • Keep service sheets, certificates and inspection evidence
  • Raise defects from PPM findings
  • Track corrective actions through to closure
  • Review repeated failures and adjust the maintenance plan
  • Complete a monthly management review of overdue work
Important: This is a general working checklist, not a substitute for site-specific risk assessment, competent-person advice, manufacturer instructions or statutory inspection requirements.
