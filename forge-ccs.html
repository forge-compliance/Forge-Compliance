What it can connect

Stop making separate systems pretend they are one.

Assets & plant

Equipment details, locations, service history, documents, photos, isolation information and asset-specific records.

PPM

Recurring maintenance, statutory tasks, due dates, owners, service records and overdue actions.

Reactive maintenance

Faults, priorities, job history, notes, images, parts, contractors and completion evidence.

Contractors

RAMS, insurance, permits, inductions, quotes, service visits and handover evidence.

Compliance evidence

Inspections, registers, SOPs, risk assessments, certificates and audit-ready records.

Commercial control

Quotes, costs, committed spend and site-specific reporting where required.
