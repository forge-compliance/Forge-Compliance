About Forge Compliance

Written for the people who actually do the work.

Forge Compliance exists to turn essential safety, maintenance and operational information into documents and systems people can actually use on a busy site.

Why Forge exists

Useful first. Professional second. Both matter.

Important maintenance and safety documentation is often either too vague to help anyone or so bloated that nobody wants to touch it. Forge was built around a simpler idea: make the work clear, controlled and defensible without burying people in paperwork.

The best document is the one that reflects the real task, the real equipment and the real pressures on the people using it. That principle sits behind everything from a single SOP to a complete custom compliance system.

Practical first

Built around real equipment, real sites and real operational pressure.

Controlled properly

Clear responsibilities, review history, training requirements, sign-off and evidence where needed.

Designed to grow

From individual documents to packs, asset information, maintenance workflows and digital tools.

What good looks like

Documentation should help the operation, not slow it down.

Depending on the job, a bespoke pack can include document control, revision history, responsibilities, PPE, emergency arrangements, training requirements, inspection records, sign-off sheets and review schedules. The point is not to add pages for the sake of it. The point is to make the important parts obvious.
