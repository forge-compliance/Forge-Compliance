About

Built from real maintenance experience

Forge Compliance exists because operational teams need documents that make sense on site, not paperwork written by someone who has never opened a plant-room door.

Practical first. Professional throughout.

Forge Compliance specialises in maintenance and facilities documentation for hotels, leisure facilities, property teams and trade contractors.

Our approach combines hands-on operational understanding with structured document control. The result is clear guidance that teams can follow, managers can approve and businesses can maintain.

Important: Clients remain responsible for verifying site-specific details, appointing competent persons and approving documents before use.

Our principles

  • Plain English over unnecessary jargon
  • Controls that match the actual task
  • Clear ownership and sign-off
  • Documents designed for review and revision
  • Honest scope and limitations
