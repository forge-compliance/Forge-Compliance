Plant room logbook checklist

A plant-room logbook should tell you what was checked, what changed and what needs attention. A ceremonial clipboard full of ticks is less ambitious.

  • Record date, time and person completing the check
  • Check access and housekeeping condition
  • Record leaks, unusual noise, vibration or smell
  • Capture relevant temperatures, pressures or gauge readings
  • Record alarms, faults or abnormal status
  • Check visible electrical panels and isolators where appropriate
  • Check chemical storage and spills where relevant
  • Raise a defect reference for abnormal findings
  • Record immediate action and responsible person
  • Link service visits and reports to the equipment
  • Keep important shutdown and isolation information accessible
  • Review recurring defects and trends
Important: This is a general working checklist, not a substitute for site-specific risk assessment, competent-person advice, manufacturer instructions or statutory inspection requirements.
