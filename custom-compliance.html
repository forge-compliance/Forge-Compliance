What Forge CCS does

One system for the things that usually end up scattered everywhere.

Most facilities and compliance teams already have the information they need. The problem is that it is split across spreadsheets, folders, WhatsApp messages, paper forms, contractor emails and somebody's memory.

Forge CCS gives that information a proper home and connects it so the maintenance and compliance picture is clear without creating another bloated system for the team to fight with.

Custom means custom. The screens, fields, statuses, permissions, locations and workflows can be built around your operation. If you do not need a feature, it does not have to be there.
