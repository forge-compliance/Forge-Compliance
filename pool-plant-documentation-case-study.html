Anonymised project example

Pool plant documentation overhaul

An anonymised example of turning pool-plant knowledge into controlled procedures, risk documentation and usable working records.

The challenge

Pool plant involves repeated operational tasks such as filter backwashing, chemical handling, equipment checks and contractor servicing. When that knowledge is mostly verbal, consistency disappears as staff change.

The Forge approach

  • Create site-specific backwash SOPs
  • Build task RAMS and risk assessments
  • Organise COSHH information for treatment chemicals
  • Introduce plant-room inspection sheets and defect tracking
  • Link asset and service information to the plant

The operational benefit

The procedure, risks and evidence sit together. Staff can follow the actual operating sequence, record completion and raise defects within the same structure.

Privacy note: This example describes the structure and workflow used in this type of project. Client-identifying information is deliberately excluded.
