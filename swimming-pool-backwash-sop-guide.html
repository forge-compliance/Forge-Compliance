Swimming pool backwash SOP checklist

A backwash procedure must match the installed filtration system and valve arrangement. Generic instructions and real plant are not always on speaking terms.

  • Identify the filter and circulation equipment
  • Record valve names and normal operating positions
  • Confirm pump stop and start requirements
  • Define the backwash valve sequence
  • Set the backwash duration or endpoint
  • Define the rinse stage
  • Record the return-to-service sequence
  • Consider water level and make-up water requirements
  • Assess pressure, chemical, electrical and slip hazards
  • Confirm normal pressure and flow after restart
  • Check for leaks or abnormal operation
  • Record the completed backwash and any defects
Important: This is a general working checklist, not a substitute for site-specific risk assessment, competent-person advice, manufacturer instructions or statutory inspection requirements.
