Hotel maintenance checklist

Hotels remain full of guests while maintenance happens, which is inconsiderate of them. This checklist helps structure the recurring engineering workload.

  • Review critical plant alarms and obvious leaks
  • Check heating and hot-water availability
  • Record guest-room and public-area defects
  • Track kitchen and operational equipment issues
  • Review pool or spa plant where applicable
  • Maintain PPM schedules by plant and area
  • Keep fire and life-safety inspection evidence current
  • Link contractor visits to the relevant assets or systems
  • Maintain room and asset repair histories
  • Escalate repeat defects rather than repeatedly resetting them
  • Keep shutdown and isolation information accessible
  • Review overdue PPM and open defects regularly
Important: This is a general working checklist, not a substitute for site-specific risk assessment, competent-person advice, manufacturer instructions or statutory inspection requirements.
