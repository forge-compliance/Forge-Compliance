Anonymised project example

Hotel maintenance and compliance system

An anonymised project example showing how a complex hospitality site can move from scattered maintenance information toward one structured operational system.

The challenge

Hotel engineering spans guest rooms, public areas, kitchens, plant rooms, leisure facilities and specialist contractors. Information can easily become split between spreadsheets, paper files, service reports and individual memory.

The Forge approach

  • Build a location hierarchy covering rooms, plant and operational areas
  • Link assets to locations and maintenance history
  • Connect PPM tasks, service records and contractor information
  • Attach SOPs, RAMS and compliance evidence to the relevant systems
  • Make defects searchable by room, issue and asset

The operational benefit

The result is a system where the maintenance team can move from a location or asset to its history, current issues, planned work and supporting evidence without hunting through multiple disconnected records.

Privacy note: This example describes the structure and workflow used in this type of project. Client-identifying information is deliberately excluded.
