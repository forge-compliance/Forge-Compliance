Facilities teams already have enough to manage without maintaining five spreadsheets, three folders, a mystery clipboard and one vital certificate that apparently vanished during a shift change. Forge builds practical controls around the way your site really works.

What Forge can organise

The working parts of FM compliance.

Built to connect maintenance activity with the records, controls and evidence behind it.

  • Planned maintenance: PPM schedules, statutory inspections, service records and overdue actions.
  • Contractor control: RAMS, insurance, permits, inductions, isolations and completion records.
  • Safe systems: Site-specific SOPs, RAMS and risk assessments for the work your team actually carries out.
  • Assets & plant: Registers, servicing, shutdown information, locations and inspection evidence.
  • Checks & evidence: Daily, weekly and monthly checks, defects, actions and management review.
  • Forge CCS: Digital control of assets, PPM, maintenance, contractors, costs and compliance evidence.

Facilities compliance FAQ

Common questions

What is facilities management compliance?

It is the practical control and evidence used to show that maintenance, inspections, contractors, risks and statutory duties are being managed consistently across a site. The exact requirements depend on the premises, equipment and activities involved.

Can Forge build site-specific FM compliance documents?

Yes. Forge can prepare site-specific SOPs, RAMS, risk assessments, registers, PPM documentation and working packs around the equipment, hazards and operating processes used on your site.

Can Forge help manage ongoing compliance paperwork?

Yes. Managed Support can help maintain and update working documentation, while Forge CCS can provide a custom digital system for assets, PPM, maintenance, contractors and evidence.
